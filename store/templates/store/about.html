Welcome to Kinmel

Kinmel Pvt. Ltd. is a Nepali e-commerce company based in Kathmandu, Nepal. Founded in 2023, the company started with a focus on affordable electronics before expanding into fashion, lifestyle, and home essentials.

Kinmel aims to make online shopping simple and accessible for customers across Nepal, offering a wide range of products with reliable delivery and customer-friendly service. The platform continues to grow its catalog, partnering with local and international sellers to bring quality products at competitive prices to shoppers nationwide.
